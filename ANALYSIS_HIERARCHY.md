## Primary analysis

The primary estimand is the shared treatment-by-adult-weight interaction in the unselected, sex-specific Cox models containing all eligible randomized ITP arms. Adult weight is standardized within treatment group, site and sex.

## Secondary analyses

Secondary analyses evaluate whether moderation differs between weight-lowering and non-lowering interventions; repeat the class comparison using only weight measured before dosing; describe matched pre-treatment weight quartiles; and assess consistency across individual compounds.

## Supporting analyses

Comparator-state, developmental-weight, longitudinal weight-gain, maternal, body-composition and cross-study analyses provide context and mechanism-oriented support.

## Sensitivity analyses

Sensitivity analyses include alternative quartile standardization, shared-control clustering in the comparator analysis, successful-arm landmarks, stratum-consistency checks and the weight-loss threshold used in the cross-study analysis. Exact eligibility rules and model specifications are given in the manuscript Methods and executable scripts.
